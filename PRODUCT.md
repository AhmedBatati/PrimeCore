# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

PrimeCore serves Arabic-first, RTL users who are evaluating premium technology purchases and local technical services. They browse phones, laptops, accessories, internet packages, Starlink/fiber solutions, and computer/laptop services, often from mobile screens and with a need for quick trust signals before contacting the store.

The primary audience wants a curated, confident storefront rather than a crowded marketplace. They need to understand what PrimeCore offers, feel that the brand is technically competent, and move smoothly toward WhatsApp/contact actions.

Internal owner/admin users also exist, but those workflows are secondary. The public storefront defines the default design identity.

## Product Purpose

PrimeCore exists to present premium technology products, internet connectivity options, and technical services with a distinctive Arabic RTL brand presence. Success means visitors quickly perceive PrimeCore as premium, trustworthy, bold, technically capable, and easy to contact.

The site should make products, packages, and services feel selected and intentional rather than dumped into a generic electronics catalog. The owner dashboard supports managing that public catalog, but the main product experience is the customer-facing brand surface.

## Brand Personality

فاخر، موثوق، جريء.

In English: premium, trustworthy, bold.

The interface should feel technical, assertive, confident, and distinctive. PrimeCore should not look ordinary or overly safe. It should use sharp hierarchy, confident spacing, memorable page presence, and carefully controlled cyan/blue accents to create a premium tech-store identity.

## Anti-references

PrimeCore should not look like:

- A cheap or crowded marketplace.
- A generic Bootstrap or template-based website.
- A childish gaming or neon interface.
- A cold foreign SaaS dashboard.
- A random AI-generated gradient design.
- A boring ordinary electronics store.
- A weak, soft, or overly safe design.
- A low-contrast gray website with weak hierarchy.
- A cluttered, noisy, or visually inconsistent site.

Avoid visual decisions that feel generic, decorative without purpose, or disconnected from an Arabic-first premium technology brand. The identity should remain clean, modern, RTL-polished, dark-first, restrained but bold, confident, and visually distinctive.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with premium confidence.
   Every public page should feel selected, composed, and intentional. Avoid marketplace clutter and template-like density.

2. Make trust visible before asking for contact.
   Strong hierarchy, clear product/service framing, readable details, and consistent interaction patterns should make WhatsApp/contact actions feel safe and natural.

3. Treat Arabic RTL as first-class.
   Layout, spacing, line length, navigation, labels, and responsive behavior should feel designed for Arabic users, not merely translated.

4. Be restrained but not timid.
   Cyan/blue accents, dark-first surfaces, and premium contrast should be used deliberately. The brand can be bold without becoming noisy or childish.

5. Preserve operational clarity.
   Public pages can be expressive, but catalog cards, package details, forms, and owner workflows must stay legible, predictable, and easy to scan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive controls in both dark and light modes. Maintain clear focus states, keyboard-accessible controls, readable Arabic typography, and responsive layouts that avoid horizontal scrolling on mobile. Honor reduced-motion preferences where motion is introduced or expanded.
